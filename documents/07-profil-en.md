# 7. Profile & Appearance

Click the **user pill** (your name + avatar) in the top right to open the menu.

## Editing your profile
From the **✎ Edit profile** item in the menu, you can:
- Change your **Name**.
- Set a **New password** (optional — leave blank if you don't want to change it).
- **Upload a photo** 📷 — the image you select is automatically **cropped to a square and
  downscaled** (256px) in the browser and stored at just a few KB. Use "Remove" to revert to
  your initials.

Your photo/name update **instantly** everywhere (top bar, profile menu, team list). If there's
no photo, your **initials** (e.g. "Ahmet Yıldız" → AY) are shown instead.

## MCP access keys
The **🔑 icon** in the menu generates/manages access keys for connecting your own AI tool
(Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor…) to LogLens. Details: [Chapter 11 — MCP integration](11-mcp-en.md).

## Theme
Choose **Light / Dark** theme from the menu. The choice persists in the browser.

## Language
Choose the **Turkish / English** interface language from the menu (persists in the browser,
per-user). This only changes the interface text (menus, buttons, form labels) — it does not
affect the error title/summary generated by the LLM, which is controlled by the
["Response language" setting](05-ai-ayarlari-en.md#response-language) and applies to the whole team.

## Design style
Styles that completely change the look of the interface (from the dropdown):
- **Default** — modern SaaS.
- **Neomorphism** — soft embossing, dual shadows.
- **Neo-minimalism** — flat, thin lines, generous whitespace.
- **Neo-futurism** — dark neon + glass effect.
- **Neo-classicism** — serif headings, warm ivory/gold palette.

The choice persists and works together with the light/dark theme (Neo-futurism establishes its
own dark world).

## Logging out
Use **Log out** at the bottom of the menu to end your session.
